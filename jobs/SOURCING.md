# Sourcing and verification

Discovery uses multiple source families so one board does not determine the search: employer/watch pages, public ATS boards, specialist boards, capability searches, and bounded aggregators. Source allocation is an effort guide, not a candidate quota.

An aggregator can reveal a lead but does not establish employer identity, availability, geography or original publication date. Promote a lead only after checking the full employer or authoritative ATS posting and its application destination.

Record provenance for first discovery and later observations. Preserve blocked and skipped checks. Never guess ATS tenants, enumerate private endpoints, infer closure from one failed page, or subscribe to paid services without explicit approval.

The watch list starts with companies approved during onboarding. Add companies based on verified search evidence and user preferences. A rejected role does not ban an entire company.
