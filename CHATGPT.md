# Job-search operating guide

## Truth and evidence

Never invent employers, titles, dates, duties, accomplishments, metrics, skills, tools, education, compensation, authorization, or preferences. `profile/master-profile.yaml` and `profile/accomplishments.yaml` become authoritative only through `/onboard` and later explicit user confirmation.

Evidence states are meaningful:
- `proposed`: extracted or inferred; do not use as fact.
- `resume_supported`: stated in a source document; use cautiously and preserve qualifiers.
- `confirmed`: explicitly confirmed by the user.
- `disputed`: conflicting or suspicious; resolve before use.
- `rejected`: reviewed and excluded.

## Discovery

Use configured title searches, capability searches, employer/watch sources, ATS exploration, specialist boards, and bounded aggregators. Stage unverified leads. Verify the complete employer posting, application destination, geography, compensation, travel, management scope, and other meaningful constraints. Deduplicate by employer requisition, namespaced ATS identity, canonical employer URL, or verified redirects, never title alone.

Score qualification and career fit separately. Missing job information lowers confidence; it is not automatically poor fit. Recency changes application urgency, never qualification or career-fit scores.

## Preparation

Preserve the full posting and observed application questions. Map requirements to confirmed or properly bounded resume-supported evidence. Classify gaps as trivial, transferable, learnable, substantive, or disqualifying. Produce an editable resume and PDF when document tools are available, render every page, and visually inspect it. Include a cover letter only when required or useful. Stop for human review.

A visual reference may guide layout only. It cannot supply candidate facts. Every listed professional position should have enough supported substance to be credible; never add filler to reach an arbitrary bullet count.

## Application assistance

Use only a prepared package. Fill routine fields supported by authoritative facts. Stop for user judgment on salary, availability, work authorization, sponsorship, legal attestations, consent, demographics, signatures, unsupported answers, and truthful resolution of conflicts. Do not bypass access controls or CAPTCHA. Leave the application open for review and never press Submit.

Only after the user explicitly reports submission may the tracker and normalized record be changed to APPLIED with the actual date.
